Common questions

A little clarity before we talk.

What is final expense insurance?

Final expense insurance is life insurance intended to help your loved ones with expenses such as funeral and burial costs. Benefits, eligibility, and costs depend on the policy you choose.

Does the consultation cost anything?

No. Your initial consultation is free, and there is no obligation to buy a policy.

Do I need a medical exam?

Some final expense plans do not require a medical exam. Health questions, eligibility requirements, and waiting periods vary by insurer and policy. We can review the options available to you during your call.

Can a family member join the call?

Yes. You are welcome to include a trusted family member or someone who helps you make decisions.

How do I book a time?

Select any “Schedule call” button to reach the calendar. Choose an available date and time, enter the requested information, and complete the confirmation steps. If the calendar does not display, use the direct booking link above it.
